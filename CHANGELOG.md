## 1.0.0

- Initial stable release of `ai_voice_orb`.
- Organic fluid physics mesh with harmonic trigonometric wave deformation.
- Interactive `OrbController` supporting states: `idle`, `listening`, `thinking`, `speaking`, `error`, and `custom`.
- Audio-reactive amplitude streaming ($0.0 \dots 1.0$) and multi-band frequency spectrum support.
- OKLab perceptual color space interpolation for vibrant, non-muddy transitions across complementary color palettes.
- Built-in theme presets: `geminiLive`, `siriHorizon`, `deepSeekNeon`, `auroraBorealis`, `amberFlame`, `monochrome`.
- GPU-accelerated CustomPainter with glowing specular highlights, ambient backdrop bloom, and neural spark particles.
- Full interactive example app with real-time state switcher, mic simulation, and theme gallery.
